Daily Minimum

Three movements, every day. Each day has a rep floor you must hit. Easy and hard variations rotate through the week so you recover without skipping days. The floor rises across four phases, and you retest at the end of each one.

Rules

Every set

  • Stop 1–3 reps short of failure. Hard, never sloppy.
  • Full range, controlled lowering, feel the target muscle work.
  • Rest 60–90 s early on, about 2 min from Build onward.

Hitting the floor

  • The rep number is a minimum. Extra sets are fine on easy days only.
  • Busy day: split the sets across morning and evening. It still counts.
  • Over 45 minutes on paper? Do two blocks rather than one long session.

When to back off

  • Joint or elbow pain (not muscle soreness): do the easy variation at the floor and nothing more for 3 days.
  • Sick or wrecked: one easy set of each still keeps the streak.
  • Pull days strain elbows first. That's why pull targets run 15% lower.

Warm-up, 4 min

  • 30 s arm circles each way
  • 10 scap push-ups, 10 scap pulls or rows
  • 10 slow squats, 5 lunges each leg
  • One easy set of the day's push variation
